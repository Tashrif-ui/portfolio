About

I’m a Computer Science & Engineering graduate who joined Codemoly as an intern and stayed on to build client products full time. Since February 2026 I’ve shipped over 300 commits across five production codebases.

Most of that is business software with real weight behind it — a garments ERP running a factory’s procurement and shipment workflow, an ads platform where I wrote the majority of the codebase, a multi-vendor marketplace. I work in Next.js and TypeScript with Drizzle or Prisma over PostgreSQL, and I tend to end up owning dashboards, reporting and the admin surfaces where a lot of the real complexity hides.

Outside client work I’m interested in applied machine learning — multi-agent systems, medical image segmentation and predictive modelling. I like problems where good engineering actually changes the outcome, and I’m open to roles where I can keep building at that level.
